Before coding, read `CLAUDE.md`, relevant requirements, architecture, ADRs, contracts, and the assigned task. Stay in scope and respect ownership. Use recorded interfaces; flag or formally propose any deviation instead of silently changing it.

After coding, run practical checks, update task state, and record changed files, factual evidence, and residual risks. Do not claim untested behavior works.
